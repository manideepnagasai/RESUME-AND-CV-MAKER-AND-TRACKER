The Resume Field Guide

How recruiters read, what ATS parses, and how MNCs, startups, product companies and service firms differ. Read once, then use the checklist before every application.

1 BUILD master 2 TAILOR to JD 3 SAVE version 4 APPLY & log 5 FOLLOW UP

01How your resume actually gets read

Two readers: a machine, then a tired human.

Pass 1 — ATS. Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, iCIMS parse your file into fields and filter by keywords. Weird tables, images-as-text or odd headings parse wrong and you are filtered out unseen.

Pass 2 — recruiter skim. Six to eight seconds: name, current title, previous title, dates, skills, bold numbers.

Pass 3 — hiring manager. Only after the skim; now bullets and metrics decide.

02Anatomy

  • Contact: name largest, title, email, phone, city, LinkedIn, GitHub. Skip photo, street address, DOB.
  • Summary: years and role, two to three JD-matched strengths, one numbered proof, what you seek.
  • Skills: grouped, comma separated, JD spelling. No bars.
  • Experience: reverse chronological, Month YYYY dates, three to five bullets per recent role.
  • Education: degree, institution, years, CGPA if strong or junior.
  • CV adds: Publications, Talks, Teaching, Scholar/ORCID, research interests — the CV toggle unlocks all of them.

03The bullet formula

Action verb + what you built or did + measurable result
Weak — duty"Responsible for managing the backend API and helping with database work."
Strong — impact"Redesigned the checkout API (Node.js, Redis caching), cutting p95 latency 61% and handling 3x Diwali traffic with zero downtime."

05ATS-safe formatting

  • Single column preferred; standard fonts; standard headings; no tables, text boxes or icons carrying information.
  • Dates as "Mar 2022 – Present"; mirror JD keywords exactly.
  • PDF unless the portal demands DOCX — direct PDF download mirrors your saved template, colours, fonts, margins and spacing.
  • Copy-text test: the Text view should read in order when pasted into notepad.

06Length, layout, pages

  • Resume: one page under about seven years, else two max. CV: two to four pages normal.
  • The Pages preview shows exact A4 pages with exact print margins — reorder sections or tighten spacing in the Design panel if a break lands badly.
  • Margins at least 0.5 inch on every page; body 10 to 12 pt.

07Mistakes that get resumes rejected

  • One generic resume blasted at two hundred jobs.
  • Duty bullets with zero outcomes.
  • Infographic templates, skill bars, cliches, first person.
  • Sending a CV where a tailored resume is expected, or vice versa.
  • File named resume_final_v7_REAL.pdf.

08Version and tracker workflow

  1. Build a master per document type (Resume, CV).
  2. Save Master versions with comments (Save version button or Ctrl+S, from anywhere).
  3. Per job: load master, tailor, save a version named after the company.
  4. Log in tracker with the version used plus a follow-up date.
  5. Weekly: clear overdue follow-ups, read the response rate.
  6. Backup JSON from Settings before big edits.
